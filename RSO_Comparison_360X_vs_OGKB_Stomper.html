What the THCV does. THCV at 1.39% on the 360X is small but not nothing. It shows up clinically as an appetite-suppressing, slightly more clear-headed modifier to THC. In a high-dose RSO context, it will not turn an indica-leaning experience into a sativa, but it can take a small bite out of the THC fog. The OGKB has no detectable THCV, so what you feel from it is unfiltered by that counterweight.

What the CBG does. Both run CBG above 2%, which is meaningful at RSO doses. CBG is mood-supportive, mildly anxiolytic, and contributes to the body-relief side of the entourage effect. It is one of the reasons full-spectrum oil feels different from pure distillate at the same THC level.

04 · PREDICTED EFFECTS

What you will actually feel.

At RSO doses these are not subtle products. Even a rice-grain serving at ~80% THC delivers more cannabinoid than a heavy session of flower. The terpene stack determines the texture of that experience, and the two profiles steer in noticeably different directions.

360X

FUNCTIONAL · HEAD-FORWARD · STREAMLINED

Expect a cleaner, more head-centric high than typical RSO. Caryophyllene dominates the terpene fraction at nearly half of total terps, which means body relief is the leading sensation but it stays mobile. You will not feel pinned to the couch from the terpene side of the equation; that decision is being made entirely by the THC dose you take.

The THCV is small but present. At sublingual doses where the effect lasts hours, that 1.39% has time to make itself known: slightly more alert, slightly less appetite, a touch less foggy than the cannabinoid number alone would suggest.

Linalool and humulene round out the back end. Calming, but not enough to drag the experience into pure sleep territory.

Body Relief Anti-Inflammatory Functional Pain Less Munchies

OGKB Stomper

DEEP · BODY-HEAVY · SLEEP-LEANING

This is a classic evening RSO experience with the volume turned up. Caryophyllene leads at 1.66%, but the supporting cast is what makes it. Linalool at nearly 1% and myrcene near half a percent stack on each other for a real body-down, mind-quiet pull. Nerolidol piles on more sedation. The Grape Gas x OG Kush Breath lineage tracks with this completely.

Flavor will be noticeably better. Sweet, fruity blueberry on the front with a skunky, earthy gas finish. That matters with RSO since most people put it under the tongue or in a capsule and the terp note hangs around.

No THCV to clear the head. The 4.72% terpene total at this composition is the closest thing to a "true indica" RSO experience on the CT shelf right now.

06 · DOSING NOTES

Read the syringe before you squeeze.

Both are 1mL syringes, both are marked in 0.05mL increments along the barrel. The math is roughly the same: the 360X delivers about 813mg of THC across the syringe, the OGKB about 766mg. A 0.05mL dose puts you in the 38 to 41mg THC range either way, which is heavy for anyone not titrated up.

START
Rice grain
A grain-of-rice dose is roughly 0.025mL, or 19 to 20mg THC. Suitable for an established medical patient with tolerance.
STANDARD
0.05mL line
One marked tick on the barrel. About 38 to 41mg THC. A real dose with hours of effect. Take 60+ minutes before peak need.
HOLD
Sublingual
Hold under tongue 60 to 90 seconds for fastest onset (20 to 40 min). Swallowing only is slower and stronger peak (60 to 120 min).

Heat tip · if the syringe is stiff, run it under warm tap water for 30 seconds. Do not microwave. RSO loses terpene volume to heat over time, especially the OGKB, which has more to lose.

08 · THE VERDICT

Different jobs.
Pick the right one.

If forced to pick one as the "better" product on paper, it is the OGKB Stomper. A 4.72% terpene total on an RSO is rare and the composition is intentional, evening-leaning, full-spectrum in a way that justifies the format. The flavor will be noticeably better and the entourage effect more pronounced. Three extra dollars for more than double the terpene content is the value bet of the two.

But the 360X is not a worse product, it is a different one. The THCV is genuinely uncommon on a CT RSO and the caryophyllene-dominant terp stack with lighter sedation makes it a better daytime micro-dosing tool. If the use case is pain management during waking hours, or filling capsules for a chronic-condition routine, the 360X is the smarter buy.

Bottom line

OGKB Stomper for nights. 360X for the rest of the day.

If only buying one and the priority is sleep or evening unwind: OGKB Stomper. If the priority is functional pain management, capsule production, or a daytime-tolerant baseline: 360X. At the $75 deal threshold a Budr seltzer comes free with either, so if you can stretch to both, get both. They are not redundant.
